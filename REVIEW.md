## What was examined and confirmed NOT to be a defect

1. **Calendar dates and shift times stored as plain strings (`YYYY-MM-DD` and `HH:MM`)**:
   `DATA_MODEL.md` explicitly notes that calendar dates and shift times of day are strings on purpose. Converting a date into an instant forces an arbitrary timezone, whereas attendance dates and shifts represent abstract local calendar concepts in IST.
2. **MongoDB storing instants as BSON UTC datetimes**:
   PyMongo returns datetimes for BSON dates. This is the expected design; timezone-aware conversions and millisecond serialization must occur at the API boundary.
3. **Absence of a synthetic primary key (`_id`) in attendance endpoints**:
   The domain addresses attendance records strictly by the composite natural key `(emp_code, date)`. Excluding `_id` and `id` from the public contract is intentional.
